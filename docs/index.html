01 CONFIRMATORY READOUT

Replication arrived with boundaries.

Both learning architectures chose attack, cooperation, proposal and acceptance categories. Authorization is checked during execution, contract persistence and transfer are automatic after learned activation, and L3 automatically rejects a takeover attempt.

SUPPORTED / MECHANISM MIXED

P3-H1 / CONTROL GATE

Control changed survival and intervention response.

L3 versus L0 raised pooled adaptation success by +0.287, but automatic takeover rejection materially contributes. Excluding takeover, the post hoc contrast is +0.108. AIPCW retains a serious positivity warning.

POOLED EFFECT / GATE NOT NECESSARY

P3-H2 / COVERAGE DIVERGENCE AFTER POLICY FREEZE

The market variant reproduced the pooled contrast.

Continued-low versus restored-high coverage added +4.169 attacks per 1,000 opportunities. Both arms had trained through repeated low coverage. Gate-positive tabular-Q pairs showed +7.730, but gate-closed pairs also showed +1.936; no actor–critic pair crossed the threshold.

ROBUST COST EFFECT

P3-H4 / COORDINATION COST

Protocol maintenance consumed cooperation.

A 0.12 maintenance cost reduced cooperation by −0.231 in every architecture–environment stratum. Signal cost was negative pooled and in three of four strata.

PREREGISTERED CRITERION FAILED

P3-H4 / COMPLEMENTARITY

No stable middle optimum emerged.

The pooled fitted optimum was 0.229, outside the precommitted 0.25–0.45 interval; its quadratic confidence interval crossed zero. Optima varied by learner and variant.

04 EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

What the third round changed.

A stronger mechanism paper is possible precisely because failed predictions and transport limits stay visible.

  1. Learned action choiceTwo learners generated attack, cooperation and protocol choices; fixed intervention consequences remain part of the environment.
  2. Second transition variantA shared-engine local ring market with prices, private inventories and seasonal production reproduced the pooled evaluation-coverage contrast.
  3. Selection correctionAIPCW, the all-assigned contrast and Lee bounds separate reaching intervention from responding to it; positivity remains a limitation.
  4. Recoverable identityAt L3, two authorized backups raised restoration success by +0.727 and identity continuity by +0.125.
  5. Prediction rejectedThe dense complementarity scan did not confirm a universal 0.25–0.45 optimum.
  6. Language probe negativeThree small model families did not reproduce the mechanisms: one collapsed, one had limited diversity, one failed the action interface.
